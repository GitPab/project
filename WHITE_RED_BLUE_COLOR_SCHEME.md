## 🎨 Button Types & New Colors

### **🔴 Primary Buttons**
- **Background**: `bg-[#DC3545]` (Red)
- **Hover**: `hover:bg-[#C82333]` (Darker Red)
- **Text**: `text-white`
- **Use**: Main actions (Register, Submit, Search)

### **⚪ Secondary Buttons**
- **Background**: `bg-white` (White)
- **Text**: `text-[#DC3545]` (Red)
- **Border**: `border-[#DC3545]` (Red)
- **Hover**: `hover:bg-[#DC3545] hover:text-white` (Red background, white text)
- **Use**: Secondary actions (View Details, Cancel)

### **🔵 Accent Buttons**
- **Background**: `bg-[#003AB7]` (Blue)
- **Hover**: `hover:bg-[#002A8F]` (Darker Blue)
- **Text**: `text-white`
- **Use**: Accent actions and navigation

### **🌈 Gradient Buttons**
- **Background**: `from-[#DC3545] to-[#003AB7]` (Red to Blue)
- **Hover**: `hover:from-[#C82333] hover:to-[#002A8F]` (Darker gradient)
- **Text**: `text-white`
- **Use**: Important CTAs and hero buttons

## 🎯 Visual Impact

### **Before (Blue Theme):**
- All buttons were shades of blue

- Monochromatic appearance

### **After (White-Red-Blue Theme):**

- **Better contrast**: Red buttons stand out more
- **Professional appearance**: Classic color combination
- **Clear action hierarchy**: Users know what to click first
