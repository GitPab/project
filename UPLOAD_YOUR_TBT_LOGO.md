# 🖼️ Upload Your TBT Logo Image

## 📁 What You Need to Do

The TBTLogo component is already configured and ready to use your actual logo image. You just need to replace the placeholder file.

### **Step 1: Locate the Placeholder**
- **File**: `public/img/tbt-logo.png` (currently a placeholder)
- **Size**: 139 bytes (placeholder file)

### **Step 2: Replace with Your Image**
1. **Take your uploaded TBT logo image**
2. **Rename it to**: `tbt-logo.png` 
3. **Replace the file at**: `public/img/tbt-logo.png`

### **Step 3: Verify**
After replacing the file:
1. **Build**: `npm run build`
2. **Preview**: `npm run preview`
3. **Check**: All TBT logos should show your image

## 🎯 Component is Ready

The TBTLogo component is already configured to:
- ✅ Use `/img/tbt-logo.png` as the image source
- ✅ Display your logo in all sizes (sm, md, lg, xl)
- ✅ Show fallback text if image fails to load
- ✅ Maintain aspect ratio with `object-contain`
- ✅ Work across all pages (onboarding, student, admin, footer)

## 🌐 Where Your Logo Will Appear

Once you upload the image, it will automatically appear in:
- ✅ **Onboarding page header**
- ✅ **University info page header**
- ✅ **Student/admin layout sidebars**
- ✅ **Mobile headers**
- ✅ **Footer sections**

## 💡 Recommended Image Specs

For best results:
- **Format**: PNG (with transparent background)
- **Size**: 200x200px or larger
- **Aspect Ratio**: Square (1:1)
- **Quality**: High resolution for sharp display
